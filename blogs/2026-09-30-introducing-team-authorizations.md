Team Authorizations let you create and manage API tokens and OAuth authorizations at the team and team’s resources level instead of tying them to one person’s account. Until now, every API token belonged to an individual. When that person left the team, changed roles, or lost access, any integration, CI/CD pipeline, or automation built on their token could break without warning, and nobody else could easily see what was at risk.
